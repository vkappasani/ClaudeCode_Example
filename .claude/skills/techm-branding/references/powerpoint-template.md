# Tech Mahindra PowerPoint Template

Use this reference when creating, redesigning, or reviewing a Tech Mahindra
PowerPoint presentation. It is derived from **TechM PowerPoint Template &
Guidelines, 23-Oct-2025**. Treat the official `.pptx` as the source of truth for
master geometry, protected areas, and placeholder positions.

## Brand intent

The presentation language is confident, modern, editorial, spacious, and
human. Create impact through message hierarchy, disciplined composition,
authentic imagery, and restrained red—not decoration. Avoid dense slideware,
ornamental effects, arbitrary shapes, and multicolour "consulting rainbow"
visuals.

## Start from the official master

- Use the 16:9 official PowerPoint file and select the closest existing layout.
- Do not rebuild recurring layouts manually or move master artwork to gain room.
- Preserve the top-right brand-flag zone, footer, copyright line, and page number.
- Keep all editable content inside the workspace demonstrated by the template.
- If content does not fit, edit or split it; do not shrink body copy below the
  master's intended size.
- Do not assume client co-branding. Confirm whether the deck is TechM-led,
  co-branded, or client-branded before adding client colours or logos.

The master includes cover, agenda, chapter divider, statement, one/two/three
column, image-and-copy, portrait grid, image-caption, data, table, video, and
closing patterns. Reuse these patterns to maintain rhythm across the deck.

## Typography

Use Aptos and the master styles. Maintain the following hierarchy where the
selected layout does not already enforce it:

| Role | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Cover or chapter-divider title | Aptos Thin/Light, approximately 60 pt |
| Page title | Aptos Thin/Light, approximately 40 pt |
| Subtitle | Aptos Thin/Light, approximately 18 pt |
| Statement or annotation | Aptos, approximately 20–24 pt |
| Body copy | Aptos Regular, approximately 16 pt |
| Supporting label | Aptos, approximately 10–13 pt |
| Source/footer | Aptos Thin/Light, approximately 7–8 pt |

- Write short, conclusion-led slide titles rather than topic labels.
- Avoid all-caps paragraphs, excessive weight changes, and multiple competing
  type sizes.
- Use one dominant message and no more than three primary information groups on
  a normal content slide.

## Colour use

Use only the approved palette in `SKILL.md`. Apply colour semantically:

- Mahindra Rising Red highlights the principal action, number, or message.
- Impact Red supports branded covers and major transitions.
- Blueprint Navy provides serious strategic or technical contrast.
- Clarity Grey backgrounds provide warm whitespace.
- Strong red should normally be reserved for the most important element.
- Do not copy incidental or legacy colours from editable sample objects when
  they conflict with the explicitly documented palette.
- Never add gradients, shadows, bevels, textures, or unapproved tints.

## Photography direction

Use authentic, lifelike photography of real people engaged in real activities.
Prefer genuine Tech Mahindra, client, or project photography over generic stock.
When stock is necessary, choose candid, context-rich, naturally lit scenes that
feel documentary rather than staged.

Avoid handshake clichés, people pointing at blank screens, exaggerated
boardroom enthusiasm, generic futuristic-tech imagery, heavy colour filters,
obviously synthetic people, and overly polished advertising photography.

### Image composition and cropping

- Use the image placeholders and their aspect ratios; never stretch an image.
- Crop decisively and preserve breathing room around faces and essential detail.
- Place the subject away from overlaid text and protected brand areas.
- When possible, orient a person's gaze or movement toward the slide content,
  not out of the frame.
- Maintain consistent crop logic and aspect ratios within image grids.
- Prefer a single dominant image over several equally competing images.
- Add captions and sources when the image requires attribution or explanation.

Approved template patterns include a full-height right-half image, horizontal
panorama, two vertically stacked images beside copy, two equal captioned images,
three equal image/portrait panels, and a full-bleed photographic background.

### Photo-stack treatment

The core template uses structured grids and split-screen compositions rather
than scrapbook-style stacks. If a narrative genuinely benefits from a photo
stack:

- Use no more than two or three photographs.
- Keep clean rectangular crops with one image clearly dominant.
- Use only small positional offsets and no rotation, or exceptionally subtle
  rotation when approved by the presentation owner.
- Do not use drop shadows, Polaroid borders, tape, torn edges, or decorative
  scrapbook effects.
- Separate images with whitespace or a thin approved-colour divider.

## Iconography and diagrams

- Use one family of flat, monoline Microsoft icons.
- Keep stroke, size, arrangement, and colour consistent.
- Prefer Ink Black or Mahindra Rising Red.
- Do not use gradients, textures, shadows, bevels, 3D icons, emoji-style art, or
  mixed icon families.
- Make diagrams editorial and grid-aligned; colour should clarify meaning rather
  than decorate.

## Story and slide rhythm

A strong deck should alternate intentionally among executive statements,
evidence, diagrams, imagery, and synthesis. Use chapter dividers for major
transitions and close each major section with a conclusion or recommendation.
Animation, when needed, should reveal narrative sequence—not decorate the page.

## PowerPoint quality gate

Before delivery, verify:

- Every slide uses or faithfully follows an approved master layout.
- Protected workspace and brand-flag areas remain clear.
- Footer, copyright, and page numbering are intact and consistent.
- No text overflows, clips, or becomes too small.
- Typography follows the intended hierarchy and uses Aptos.
- Every colour belongs to the approved palette and accessible contrast is kept.
- Photographs are authentic, correctly cropped, and high enough resolution.
- Image grids have consistent crop logic, alignment, and spacing.
- Icons are flat, monoline, and from one family.
- Charts show accurate scales, units, legends, baselines, and sources.
- Client logos and colours appear only under an agreed co-branding approach.
