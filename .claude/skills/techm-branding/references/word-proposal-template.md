# Tech Mahindra Word Proposal Template

Use this reference when creating, redesigning, or reviewing a Tech Mahindra
proposal document. It is derived from the official **TechM Word Proposal
Template** (5-page master). Treat the official `.docx` as the source of truth
for margins, styles, and placeholder positions; this file supplies the
decision rules for using it correctly.

## Document structure

A TechM proposal follows this fixed page order — do not reorder or skip
sections:

1. **Cover page**
2. **Confidentiality agreement**
3. **Table of contents**
4. **Numbered content sections** (Section 1, 1.1, 1.1.1, Section 2, 2.1, …)

## Cover page

- Full-bleed black-and-white architectural photograph (upward-looking
  skyscraper shot) fills the top ~60% of the page.
- TechM logo (full color lockup) sits over the photo, top-left.
- A horizontal bar directly beneath the photo is split Mahindra Rising Red
  (left ~2/3) and Steel Grey (right ~1/3).
- Below the bar, left column: "Tech Mahindra Proposal" in Impact Red
  (Aptos), then the actual `<Enter Proposal Title>` in large black Aptos,
  then `<Enter Date>` in italic grey.
- Right column: a bordered placeholder box labeled "Customer Logo" — insert
  the customer's logo here once known; never leave the customer's actual name
  substituted into the TechM lockup or vice versa.
- Never distort, recolor, or reposition the TechM logo on the cover.

## Confidentiality agreement (page 2)

- Left ~40% of the page is a solid Impact Red panel containing the heading
  "CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT" in white uppercase Aptos, with a photographic
  image bleeding out from behind it on the right edge.
- Below the panel, on a Clarity Grey 1 ground, place the standard
  confidentiality paragraph. Use this exact approved language, substituting
  only the bracketed customer name:

  > The information contained herein is proprietary to Tech Mahindra Limited,
  > hereby referred to as **TechM** and may not be used, reproduced, or
  > disclosed to others except as specifically permitted in writing by TechM.
  > It has been made available to \<Customer\>, hereby referred to as
  > \<Customer\>, solely for an objective evaluation of TechM's services, and
  > such information may be disclosed only to those employees of \<Customer\>,
  > who have the need to know such information in order to perform the
  > evaluation.
  >
  > This document shall remain valid and open for acceptance for a period of
  > **120** days from the above date of submission to \<Customer\>.

- The **120** days figure is the standard validity window — change it only if
  the deal team has explicitly agreed a different period, and keep it bolded.
- Below a horizontal rule, add a contact block: TechM Account Manager Name
  (in Mahindra Rising Red), Designation, Email, Contact Number — plain text,
  no logo.
- Never place body text over the red panel or over the bleeding photograph.

## Table of contents (page 3)

- Centered uppercase heading "TABLE OF CONTENTS" in Impact Red.
- Two-level entries (`1. Section` bold, `1.1 Sub-Section` regular), each with
  a dotted leader to a right-aligned page number.
- Keep this auto-generated from heading styles — never hand-type page numbers.

## Content sections (page 4+)

- Section number + title (`1. Section 1`) as Heading 1, in Impact Red Aptos.
- Sub-section (`1.1 Sub-Section 1`) as Heading 2, in Impact Red Aptos, smaller
  than Heading 1.
- Sub-sub-section (`1.1.1 Sub Section 3`) as Heading 3, in black Aptos.
- Body copy is black Aptos Regular, left-aligned (not justified), standard
  paragraph spacing.
- Diagrams, service-line graphics, and callouts referenced in body text should
  use only the approved color palette (see `SKILL.md`) — never introduce
  unapproved accent colors into an embedded graphic.

## Footer and pagination (every content page after the cover)

- Left: `Copyright © <year> Tech Mahindra. All rights reserved.` — update the
  year at time of writing, never leave a stale year from the template.
- Right: `Page <n> of <total>` — auto-generated field, never hand-typed.
- Keep the top-right brand-flag/logo zone clear on every content page.

## Word quality gate

Before delivery, verify:

- Page order is cover → confidentiality → TOC → numbered sections, unchanged.
- Customer name/logo placeholders are either filled in consistently or left as
  the literal `<Customer>` / "Customer Logo" placeholders — never partially
  filled.
- The 120-day validity clause is present and intentional if changed.
- TOC page numbers are field-generated, not hand-typed, and match actual pages.
- Heading colors follow the Impact Red (H1/H2) / black (H3+) hierarchy.
- Footer shows the current year and correct auto-paginated `Page X of Y`.
- No unapproved colors appear in embedded charts or diagrams.
