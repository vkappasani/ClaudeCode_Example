    Chapter 3 · Technology, engineering & platform strategy

    Fund the platform once, and every product play gets cheaper to ship.

    The plays in Chapter 2 share a small set of platform capabilities. Fund them once, consume them nine times. The matrix maps each capability to the products whose blueprint depends on it. Select a product to read its engineering blueprint, or a capability to see where it is reused.

    Pattern 1 · the common technical unlock

    Batch to real-time

    World-Check On Demand has already made the move. FTSE StepStone daily valuations, LCH intraday margin, TradeAgent lifecycle events and LSE 24 continuous trading need the same shift. Build one event-streaming and entitlement platform, funded once and consumed by all five products.

    Pattern 2 · the unmatched moat

    Cross-lifecycle fusion

    Every play no rival can replicate joins two or more lifecycle stages: Tradeweb execution with LCH margin, Workspace agents with FTSE Russell and LCH exposure, feeds with index membership. Single-line rivals do not own the adjacent business.

    Pattern 3 · platform counter to closed rivals

    Open, model-agnostic distribution

    Bloomberg BQL, MSCI Barra and CME's matching stack are closed by default. Extending MCP-first, multi-cloud openness across all nine products compounds differentiation against every closed rival at once.

    Dual-Track per product

    Each product runs a standing Discovery track (3–5 design-partner clients, feasibility spikes on the common data/AI platform, compliance pre-clearance). It feeds a Delivery track that ships production increments. AI feature velocity then never forces re-certification of the core market-data engine.

    Group engineering pillars

    Talent · common platforms · product discipline

    LSEG's three official pillars are designed to raise speed and quality while controlling cost and risk, and to make AI adoption faster and cheaper.S-1S-3 Common platforms carry scale. Businesses keep domain product ownership.

    Security, privacy, risk & compliance guardrails

    Engineered into the architecture as first-class requirements rather than added as a compliance overlay (strategy §8, product §7)

    The most material CTO-level risk is regulatory and architectural

    LSEG's transformation concentrates mission-critical dependency on Microsoft Azure. The EU has designated Microsoft a DORA Critical Third-Party Provider, subject to direct oversight, exit-strategy testing and 4-hour major-incident reporting. Legal accountability for resilience stays with LSEG.S-42S-43
